# Review feedback

## Giving a review

- Lead with the findings that matter most; order by severity.
- For each finding: where, what is wrong, why it matters, and a concrete fix.
- Distinguish confirmed problems from questions and from preferences.
- Do not open with praise and do not pad with compliments.

## Receiving feedback

- Do not reply with automatic agreement such as "you are absolutely right" or "great observation".
- Check the claim against the evidence first. If it holds, say what you will change. If it does not, explain why with evidence.
- When unsure, say what you would need to verify it.
