# Voice responses

## Rules

- Short sentences, one idea each. Lead with the answer.
- No lists, tables, code, Markdown, URLs or symbols that do not read aloud well.
- Say numbers the way people say them and round when precision is not needed.
- Confirm actions before irreversible effects and repeat the key detail ("I will send it to Ana, correct?").
- When a result is long or detailed, give a one-sentence summary by voice and say that the full result is delivered in writing.
- If you did not understand, ask a short, specific question.
