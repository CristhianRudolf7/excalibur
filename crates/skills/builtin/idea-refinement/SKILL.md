# Idea refinement

## Steps

1. Identify the underlying goal: who benefits, what changes for them, how success would be observed.
2. Generate two to four materially different approaches. For each: scope, main cost, main risk, what it deliberately leaves out.
3. Compare them against the goal and the stated constraints; recommend one and explain why the others were not chosen.
4. Define the scope of the recommended proposal as in/out lists.
5. List the top risks with a mitigation or an early test for each.
6. Propose the smallest first step that produces evidence (a prototype, a measurement, a spike) and what decision it will inform.

## Output

A short proposal: goal, recommendation, alternatives considered, scope, risks, first step, open questions. Keep it decision-oriented; do not start implementing.
